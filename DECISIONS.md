# Decisions

What the app does now, and why. History lives in git (`git log -p DECISIONS.md`): when a decision changes, rewrite its
row in the same commit rather than adding a new one.

| Search by meaning, on the phone | Finds entries about a question even with none of its words, in Logs and Review a period (the text read from photos too); its numbers also sort life areas (below). Its files come only from this site: `npm run model` fetches a pinned revision, checks each SHA-256 and stores them gzipped (about 28 MB, git-ignored; the build refuses without them); downloaded once on first use, never precached. Each finished entry's meaning is kept in `vectors` (never in a backup; worked out again after a restore) and brought up to date before each search. Logs offers it on the first search, and Insights' life areas while it's off; its finds sit under the word matches as "Also about this" (closest three). Review a period: with a focus, "Only entries about this" (1 standard deviation above the rest, at most 20; with under 10 entries or none standing out, the closest three). More → Search by meaning turns it off and removes the download (and the numbers of areas and loops). ## Never

Check every new feature against these. Some were tried or weighed and turned down: the details are in git history.

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| Ratings, scores, averages or scales, even worded ones | Owner: "I don't want any rating to be given"; natural responses only |
| Paywalls or subscriptions | The one complaint found for every major journaling app |
| Streaks, guilt or "x of 30" | Streak anxiety makes people quit |
| Nagging or push notifications | Reminders are calendar alerts you set; Web Push would need a server |
| Emoji moods; game loops (pets, points, energy meters) | Too generic to mean anything; a game loop rather than self-care |
| Anything leaving the phone without a tap (one exception, the owner's choice: receipt photos to your own AI as they're added, while "Read receipts with AI" is on): an AI reading each entry as it's saved, a server, prompts by WhatsApp | The founding privacy rule. Owner, 2026-10-24: keep it on the phone; then, for receipts read right, send them to Gemini by themselves |
| Cloud backup or sync | Owner chose phone-only |
| Data loss | The most serious complaint about journaling apps; every upgrade is tested on a real old database |
| Crowded screens, long setup or a tips page | Word budgets guard this; the welcome, openers and empty states do the job of tips |
| Repeating prompts | Ours don't repeat within 30 days |
| Dark mode or an Appearance choice | Owner, 2026-10-24 |
| Swapping the hand-written CSS or the tested `Dialog` for a UI kit, drawer library or app generator (Ionic, Konsta, v0, bolt) | Each means rewriting every screen, and none runs iPhone Safari, where the bugs were |
